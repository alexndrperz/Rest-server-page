Our Story

A Legacy of Culinary Excellence

Founded in 1987 in the heart of the city, La Maison D'Or has been a beacon of gastronomic artistry for over three decades. Our philosophy is simple: honor the ingredients, respect tradition, and dare to innovate.

Every dish that leaves our kitchen tells a story of passion, precision, and an unwavering commitment to perfection. From our hand-selected produce to our carefully curated wine cellar, every detail is considered.

Meet the Chef

Laurent Dubois

Executive Chef & Founder

With over three decades of culinary expertise, Chef Laurent has earned recognition across the globe. Trained under the legendary masters of French haute cuisine, he brings a philosophy that balances respect for tradition with a relentless pursuit of innovation.

His creations have earned La Maison D'Or three Michelin stars and numerous international accolades. Each plate reflects his belief that food should be an emotional experience, not just a meal.
